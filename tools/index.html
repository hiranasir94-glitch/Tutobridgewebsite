For parents: using these tools safely

AI tools can help children learn, but they work best with an adult nearby.

✅ Check the age ruleMany AI chat tools ask for users to be 13 or older, or need a parent's permission. Look at the sign-up page.
🧠 Help, not homework-copyingEncourage tools that explain step by step, like Khanmigo, over ones that just give answers.
🔍 Double-check factsAI can sound confident and still be wrong. Compare with the textbook or ask the teacher.
🔒 Keep personal details privateChildren should not type their full name, school, address or photos into AI tools.
