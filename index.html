FAQ

Questions, answered

Quick answers about using Tutobridge.

How does Tutobridge work?

Search for teachers near you, chat with the ones you like, then send a trial request. When the teacher accepts, you can arrange lessons directly.

Who can see my address and phone number?

A teacher sees your address and phone number only after they accept your booking request. Teachers' exact locations are never shown, only a rounded distance.

How are teachers verified?

Teachers submit their qualifications when they register. A verified badge is shown on profiles that have been approved.

Where is Tutobridge available?

We are starting in Lahore and matching teachers at neighbourhood level. More cities will follow.

Which classes and subjects are covered?

Early years and Montessori, Primary, Matric/FSc, O Levels, A Levels and IB, across subjects such as Mathematics, English, Urdu, sciences and Computer Science.

Where do lessons take place?

Teachers choose where they teach: at a school, at the student's home, at their own home, or a mix. You can filter by this when searching.

How much do lessons cost?

Each teacher lists a fee per day or per month in PKR on their profile, so you can compare before you chat.

Do I pay inside the app?

Plans and payments are still being finalised. Lesson fees are agreed directly between parent and teacher.

How do I contact support?

Email support@tutobridge.com.
