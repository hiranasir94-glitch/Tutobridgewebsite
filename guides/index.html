How do I find a good home tutor in Lahore?

Start with your area. A tutor who lives close by is more likely to stay punctual and keep coming through the year, so search near your neighbourhood first, for example DHA, Johar Town, Model Town, Gulberg, Bahria Town or Wapda Town.

Shortlist two or three tutors who teach your child's exact class and board (Matric, O Level, A Level or IB) and the subjects you need.

Check qualifications and experience on the profile, and prefer tutors with a verified badge.

Chat before you commit. Ask how they plan lessons and track progress, then book a trial class before agreeing a monthly fee.

Home tutor, online tutor or academy: which is best?

A home tutor gives one-to-one attention in a familiar place. It suits younger children, students who need close follow-up, and families who prefer not to travel.

An online tutor works well for older students who are comfortable on a laptop. You can choose from teachers across Lahore, not just your street, and save travel time.

An academy or tuition center suits students who learn well in a group, want a fixed timetable, or are preparing for board exams with past-paper practice.

Many families mix them, for example an academy for exam practice plus a home tutor for one weak subject. Tutobridge lets you search all three: online tutors, home tutors and academies on a map.

What should I ask a tutor before the first class?

  • Which classes, boards and subjects do you teach, and for how many years?
  • How do you plan lessons, and how will you share my child's progress with me?
  • Where will lessons take place: at our home, your home, online or at an academy?
  • How many days a week, how long is each class, and what is the fee per month?
  • What happens if a class is missed, and is there a trial class?

How much do tutors charge in Lahore?

Fees vary a lot. They depend on the class level (O and A Level usually cost more than primary), the subject, the number of days per week, whether the tutor comes to your home, the area, and the tutor's experience.

How do I keep my child safe with a home tutor?

  • Meet the tutor first, ideally with a trial class while a parent is at home.
  • Prefer verified tutors and check their qualifications.
  • Keep lessons in a shared, open part of the house and stay in touch with your child about how classes are going.
  • On Tutobridge, a teacher sees your address and phone number only after accepting your booking request, and teachers' exact locations are never shown.
